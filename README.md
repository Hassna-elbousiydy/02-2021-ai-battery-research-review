## Research overview

This collaborative review examines where AI and machine learning can support battery research and where their limitations need closer scrutiny. It spans materials discovery, manufacturing, characterization, diagnosis and prognosis, as well as literature mining and other applications.

A central concern is the connection between useful predictions and trustworthy data. The review brings methods and application areas together to help researchers assess model choices, evidence and practical research opportunities.
